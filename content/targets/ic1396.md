IC 1396 is a big region of glowing hydrogen in Cepheus, about 2,400 light-years away. Almost all of it is lit by one star system near its center, HD 206267, a massive triple star whose stellar wind is among the fastest measured for stars of its type.

The Elephant's Trunk is a dense, dark globule of gas and dust that's dense enough to shield itself from that star's ultraviolet light. The bright, curling rim is the surface of the cloud being lit up and eaten away.

On the edge of the nebula is Mu Cephei, a red supergiant that is one of the largest stars known.
