The Soul is also called the Embryo Nebula and, by radio astronomers, Westerhout 5. It's usually named after the star cluster in its body, IC 1848, but several other small clusters are embedded in it too.

It sits right next to the Heart Nebula, and together they're the "Heart and Soul," part of a bigger complex of star-forming clouds.

Two galaxies, Maffei 1 and Maffei 2, sit in the same patch of sky behind it, but the Milky Way's dust hides them so well that they're very hard to see.
