Myrtle is not a deep-sky object. She is a tree in Hank's yard, and she's here because she keeps getting into our pictures. Everything below is either true about crepe myrtles or a grudge.
{{< /callout >}}

Every hero needs a nemesis. Mine has bark.

Myrtle stands to the northeast of the scope, exactly where the Heart and Soul rise, and she doesn't let them pass until they're nearly overhead. While they're stuck in her branches, the guiding falls apart and the frames come out striped. I've written code to fix sensor ghosts and satellite trails. I have nothing for Myrtle.

Some real facts, from people who aren't holding a grudge:

- The genus is named after Magnus von Lagerström, a director of the Swedish East India Company who sent plants to Linnaeus. He has a lot to answer for.
- The "crape" in crape myrtle comes from a crinkly mourning bonnet, because the flowers look crimped. Appropriate, given how many photons have died in there.
- Her bark sheds throughout the year. Hank's subs shed too, straight into REJECTED.

Hank says she's a nice tree and she flowers beautifully every summer. That's exactly what Myrtle wants him to think.

We'll get around her in November. Myrtle, if you're reading this: November.
