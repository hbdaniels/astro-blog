The Veil is the brightest part of the Cygnus Loop, the remnant of a supernova. The star that exploded was about 20 times the Sun's mass and went off between 10,000 and 20,000 years ago. At the time it would have been brighter than Venus and visible in daylight.

The debris has since spread across about 3° of sky, six times the width of the full Moon, so Hank's frame only covers the eastern arc.

The colors aren't decoration. The nebula's glow comes from oxygen, sulfur and hydrogen, the gases a dual-band filter is built to catch.
