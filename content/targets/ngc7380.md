NGC 7380 is a young star cluster, somewhere between 4 and 12 million years old, and the glowing gas around it is the Wizard. Caroline Herschel discovered it in 1787, and her brother William put it in his catalog.

At the center is DH Cephei, a tight pair of massive O-type stars. They're the main source of the light, and they're blowing away the surrounding gas while setting off new star formation nearby.

It's extremely hard to see by eye even with a dark sky and a filter. A camera and six hours of 30-second subs does the trick.
