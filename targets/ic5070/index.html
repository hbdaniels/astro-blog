IC 5070, the Pelican Nebula

CatalogIC 5070 and IC 5067
TypeEmission nebula (H II region)
ConstellationCygnus
DistanceAbout 1,800 light-years
Apparent sizeAbout 60′ × 50′, two full Moons
Best seasonSummer into early autumn

The Pelican sits near the bright star Deneb, separated from its bigger neighbor, the North America Nebula, by a lane of dark dust in front of them. Both are part of one larger glowing cloud.

Astronomers study it a lot because it’s caught in the act. Light from young stars is heating the cold gas and pushing an ionization front outward, and dense filaments of cold gas are still holding out.

It won’t stay a pelican, either. Millions of years from now the gas and stars will have moved into something completely different.
